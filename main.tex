\begin{abstract}
    Cell Segmentation as a growing field has many challenges to face. Analysis of the large datasets cannot be performed by hand. another such challenge, densely packed cells in all dimensions, has yet to be conquered universally. In this report multiple state of the art algorthims are tested on Spleen samples. an known densly packed tissue with important immunological functions. \\
    - all do struggle with the tissue \\
    - human segmentation is limited \\
    - require eval without reference \\
    - limited options so far \\
    - introduce pipeline for automated segmentation \\
\end{abstract}
