\subsection*{Benchmarking}
Computer predictions of shapes is conventionally evaluated by comparing the masks to a human-drawn ground-truth. The quantification of the prediction quality is facilitated through similarity analysis. This is naturally not constrained to cell predictions, MRI and CT predictions or financial data can be assessed using IoU and derived values. The F-measure, first arithmetic mean of precision and recall, stands as the default value and is in this report too the primary metric. Investigating the precision and recall separately can yield additional insights(, but will not be featured in this report). \\
The ground truth is either prepared by a single expert or undergoes multiple iteration of verification of variable expert-composition, e.g. crowd-sourced approach for the \textsc{TissueNet} project. These masks of course exhibit inconsistent quality, repeated analysis has shown a constraint to 0.005 inter-annotator-disagreement. \\
In evaluating LLMs  suggested to rely primarily on machine-jury/-annotators for benchmarks. This is of course not entirely applicable to medical or cell-cegmentatin. other maskless attempts have been made to deal with the increasing aptness of ML-segmentation. The PCA by CellSegmentationEvaluation, relies on metrics that apply to both the staining-image and the prediction and compares them both. the limits will be shown later. another approach is probability based, by using multiple algorithms to predict and accumulate their results in a probability map we can evaluate an algorithm without a human-annotator. \\

\begin{equation}

\subsubsection*{Jaccard-Index}
Intersection over Union
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:jaccard}
    J(SR,GT) = \frac{SR\cap GT}{SR \cup GT} = \frac{TP}{TP+FP+FN} 
\end{equation}
\subsubsection*{FMeasure}
...
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:F1}
    F1 = \frac{2}{\frac{1}{PR}+\frac{1}{RE}} = 2\frac{PR\cdot RE}{PR+RE} = \frac{2TP}{2TP+FP+FN}
\end{equation}
\subsubsection*{Dice-Sörens(?) Coefficient}
basically F1...
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:DCE}
    DCE = \frac{2|SR\cap GT|}{|SR|+|GT|} = \frac{2TP}{2TP+FP+FN}
\end{equation}
