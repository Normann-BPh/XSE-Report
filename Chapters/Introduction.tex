\section*{Introduction}
\dropcap{S}egmenation of cells or their nuclei is a highly computational field, especially with machine learning, the prevalent tool and foundation of most algorithms. The project behind this report focused on evaluating a selection of segmentation algorithms using four different benchmarks. These algorithms will receive a short introduction followed by the evaluation. In tandem the corresponding pipeline will be explained. Afterwards, a more in depth exploration of the pipeline finishes this report.
