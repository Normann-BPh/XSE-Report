\section*{Models}
Chosen were a variety of different segmentation algorithms. Some have been proven to outperform other members of this collection in certain challenges. Since the mouse spleen is a tissue type rarely discussed, and in many evaluations not thematised, assessing these algorithm gives more context to their applicability. \\
All of the following models, except ProSeg, use deep-learning. In addition to the presented algorithms, multiple SRT-utilizing algorithms were considered but deemed inadequate, either because of their incompatibility with Xenium-Output or package-conflicts that could not be resolved --- SCS, UCS and RNA2seg.\\
