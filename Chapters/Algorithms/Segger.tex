\subsection*{\color{segger}\textsc{SEGGER}}
The Graph Neural Network Segger aims to prevent typical errors by re-thinking cell segmentation as a transcript-to-cell link prediction task. Transcripts and cells are represented as nodes, with inter-transcript edges embody the spatial relation, and transcript-cell edges the assignment.
