\subsection*{\color{xenium}\textsc{Xenium}}
Xeniums provided nucleus and cell boundaries are predicted by utilizing the DAPI stains and a custom neural network. The cells are either predicted by expanding the nucleus-bounds by 5$\unit{\micro\meter}$ or until it meets another cells boundary, or, if the boundary stains are present, applying, again, a custom neural network to infer cell boundaries, alternatively, if only 18s RNA stains are available, the nucleus is expanded until the stain ends or a different cell is meet.
