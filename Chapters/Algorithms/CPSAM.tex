\subsection*{\color{cpsam}\textsc{Cellpose-SAM}}
Cellpose-SAM, essentially fourth iteration of Cellpose, is a widely used algorithm utilizing a generalized model as a backbone. It replaces the Cellpose U-net backbone with the foundational model SAM. This new model achieves \textit{superhuman generalization}, thus breaking the inter-annotator bound. \\
The U-Net backbone of the regular Cellpose model, with skip connections and global style vector, predicts the vector flows, an intermediate pixel-wise representation of the mask that point towards the cell-center. The SAM ViT-L encoder is used as a backbone to replace the encoding of the U-Net model, custom modifications | input and patch size, and global attention | allow the utilization of the SAM-weights to predict the vectorized flow representation. A majority of the SAM parameters were kept | 305M out of 312M | enabling the use of generalized [\textit{understanding of images}].
