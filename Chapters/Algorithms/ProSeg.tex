\subsection*{\color{proseg}\textsc{ProSeg}}
The most recent development in probabilistic approaches to transcript assignment and cell segmentation, applies a framework related to cellular pots models (CPM) to simulate cell-location \cite{jones_cell_2025}. CPMs replicate cell-cell interactions in an abstracted manner by assigning mismatched pairs a higher energy than matching, thus, through Monte-Carlo iterations, sorting a batch of cells by their type \cite{graner_simulation_1992}.\\
The past success of CPMs adaptability to not only cell sorting but also migration and chemotaxis as well as it's Metropolis-like sampling procedure permit conversion to a fully-probabilistic Markov-Chain-Monte-Carlo inference model for cell-gene correlation. This enables segmenting based on gene-profiles. Instead of a complete detachment from morphology-imaging data, as it suggests so far, the initialization of the model includes nuclear-segmentations. The next stage includes accommodations for possible variations in gene-expression, and further, the model assesses transcript-shifting, either by leakage or erroneous transcript-localization. The shifting-probability is limited to a user set amount.\\
Solely a table of transcript-locations with associated transcript-IDs suffices as input for \textsc{ProSeg}, with the option to include segmentation priors. No priors were provided for this analysis.\\
The output can be configured to include, among others, the count-matrix of the transcript-profiles, multi-layer cell-polygons, or a single-layer representation of the 3D segmentation.
